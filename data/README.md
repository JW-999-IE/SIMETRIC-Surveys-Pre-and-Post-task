Raw participant-level survey responses are not deposited in this repository.

They combine age band, gender, job title, clinical setting and years of clinical
experience for 27 clinicians recruited through named hospital networks. That
combination could permit re-identification of individual participants, and the
study's Data Availability statement provides that participant-level data are not
publicly deposited.

Aggregate outputs sufficient to reproduce every reported statistic are in
../results/. Raw responses may be requested from the corresponding author,
subject to ethical approval and institutional data-sharing agreements.
